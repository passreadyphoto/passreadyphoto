## PassReady

**Passport, visa and ID photo compliance — checked free, before you pay.**

[passreadyphoto.com](https://passreadyphoto.com)

Most tools resize an image to the right pixel size and stop there. Correct size is the
easiest requirement to meet and the least common reason a photo is rejected.

PassReady measures the photograph itself — head height crown to chin, eye line position,
head tilt, framing, resolution, focus, glare, lighting and background uniformity — against
the issuing authority's own published rules, and shows every value next to the range your
document requires.

- **70 documents across 62 countries**
- **50 verified directly against the issuing authority**, each page showing the date it was last checked
- The biometric audit runs **in your browser** — the photo is not uploaded to be judged
- Exact millimetre sizing at 300 DPI, plus a printable sheet

Built with Next.js, MediaPipe and sharp.

Every number there is checkable against what's shipped — that's why it's written that way.
